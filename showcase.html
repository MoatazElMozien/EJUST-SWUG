The E-JUST SolidWorks User Group (SWUG) is a community of engineering students dedicated to mastering 3D CAD design — one of the industry's most powerful mechanical design platforms.

From concept to detailed assembly, our members tackle real-world engineering challenges, collaborate on complex mechanical systems, and develop the technical skills that define tomorrow's engineers.
